Support

Memora puts your Snapchat Memories into Apple Photos, with their real dates and places. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Need a hand? Email info@creamole.de and describe what you were doing when it went wrong. If the import produced a report, include the summary at the top of it. That usually tells us most of what we need.

Getting your export from Snapchat

Memora needs the official export that Snapchat gives you. It never asks for your Snapchat password.

  1. In the Snapchat app, tap your profile icon (top left), then the Settings gear (top right).
  2. Scroll down to the Privacy Controls section and tap My Data.
  3. Turn on Export your Memories, and make sure Export JSON Files is also on. Without the JSON, the dates and locations are missing and there is nothing for Memora to restore.
  4. Choose All Time and submit the request. Snapchat lets you know when the export is ready, usually within a few hours but sometimes a day or more.
  5. Open My Data again (or the link in Snapchat's email) and download every ZIP part to the Files app on your iPhone. Keep their original names.

Importing into Apple Photos

  1. Open Memora and tap Start Migration.
  2. Tap Select Export and choose every ZIP part together.
  3. Review the scan. Memora shows how many Memories it found before anything is imported.
  4. Start the import and allow Photos access. Allow Full Access lets Memora create its album and verify the result.
  5. When it finishes, open the import report to see exactly what arrived.

Common questions

Do I need to log in to Snapchat?

No. Memora has no login of any kind. It only reads the export file you give it.

My export came as several ZIP files.

That is normal for large accounts. Select all of the parts together. Memora treats them as one export and tells you if a part is missing. The first part holds the index for the whole export, so that's the one you can't skip.

Some Memories came in with today's date.

That happens when the export has no JSON file, or when a particular Memory has no date recorded in it. Memora never invents a date: if the export does not say when a photo was taken, the photo keeps whatever date Photos gives it. Requesting the export again with Export JSON Files turned on usually fixes it.

Some Memories failed to import.

The most common cause is an expired download link. Snapchat's links stop working after a while, so an old export may no longer be able to fetch its media. Request a fresh export. The import report names every Memory that did not make it and why.

Can I run it again without creating duplicates?

Yes. Memora remembers what it has already imported and skips it, so an interrupted import can be resumed safely.

Will this delete anything from Snapchat?

No. Memora only reads your export file. It never contacts your Snapchat account and cannot change anything there.

Where did my Memories go?

Into your Apple Photos library, dated to when they were taken, so they appear in the right place in your timeline instead of all at the end. If you allowed full photo access, they are also collected in an album.

Purchases

What does Memora cost?

Your first 100 Memories import free. The Lifetime Unlock is a one-time purchase that imports the rest. There's no subscription.

I bought the Lifetime Unlock on another device, or reinstalled the app.

Open Memora, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

I have an offer code.

Redeem it in the App Store (tap your profile picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code), or open the link you were sent. Then open Memora and tap Restore Purchase in Settings.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
