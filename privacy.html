Privacy-first by design

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Memora moves your Snapchat Memories into Apple Photos. It runs entirely on your iPhone.

The short version: your Memories never leave your iPhone. There's no account, no analytics, no tracking and no server of ours. Your photos, videos, dates and locations stay on your device. The only thing that goes online is the check that confirms a Lifetime Unlock purchase (see Purchases).

What Memora accesses, and why

Your Snapchat export file

You choose it with the iOS file picker. Memora reads it to find your Memories and their dates and locations. It is never uploaded, copied off the device, or sent anywhere.

Your photo library

Memora needs permission to add your Memories to Apple Photos, and to read back what it added so it can verify the import finished correctly. It does not browse, index, or transmit the rest of your library.

Snapchat's download servers

If your export contains download links rather than the media itself, Memora fetches those files directly from Snapchat, over HTTPS, using the links Snapchat gave you. Nothing else is sent with those requests, and the files go straight to your device.

What stays on your device

  • A record of which Memories have been imported, so an interrupted import can resume and duplicates are not created
  • Your import settings and history reports
  • Temporary copies of media while an import is in progress, deleted afterwards

All of it lives in Memora's own app storage. Deleting the app removes all of it.

What Memora does not do

  • No analytics or crash-tracking SDKs that profile you
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is no data to sell
  • No Snapchat login, password, or account access
  • No Snapchat private APIs
  • No accounts and no sign-in of any kind

Purchases

If you buy the Lifetime Unlock, it is a one-time In-App Purchase handled entirely by Apple. Apple processes the payment, and Memora never sees your payment details. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, which is why it can be restored on a new device.

To check whether you own the Lifetime Unlock, Memora uses RevenueCat, a purchase-verification service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app (not your name, email or Apple ID) together with the App Store transaction details for this purchase. It is used only to confirm the unlock and restore it on reinstall. It is never used for advertising or tracking, and none of your photos, videos or export files are sent. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

App updates

Memora checks for small app updates through Expo Application Services. That check sends the app's version and platform so the right update can be delivered. It contains nothing about you or your Memories.

iCloud

If you have iCloud Photos turned on, Apple syncs your imported Memories to iCloud the same way it syncs everything else in your library. That is your device setting, between you and Apple. Memora doesn't control it and can't see it.

Children

Memora is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because Memora holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on our side. Everything is on your device and under your control. Deleting the app deletes everything Memora stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
